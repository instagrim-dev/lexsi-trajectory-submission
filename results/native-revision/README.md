# Native decision-revision witness

On November 10, 2026, a retained BMO Workbench session handled one synthetic invoice-policy case. The operator supplied the decision and checks. Local d1-3B answered three specified concern questions, and Tiny Aya generated complete JSON candidates through a constrained study adapter. The seven-day candidate was checked, reviewed, approved and accepted. After the operator revised the decision to 14 days, its application was refused with HTTP 409 and the source hash remained unchanged. After a BMO restart with a reporting-selection fix, a newly generated 14-day candidate was checked, approved and applied; the post-application check passed. The same checker was used for proposal qualification and the post-application check.

This single witness does not establish automatic ambiguity discovery, general reliability, or superiority over a generic workflow. It is separate from the fine-tuned adapter experiment. The adapter constrained Aya to a flat JSON output; the models did not author the decisions or checker. The reporting-selection fix and restart occurred between the stale refusal and the final successful construction.

The retained records are the [live model inputs and outputs](live-model-calls.jsonl), [decisions](rerun-decisions-final.json), [original proposal](rerun-proposal-1-final.json), [fresh proposal](rerun-proposal-2-final.json), [application observations](application-http-observations.json), [before](source-before-revision.sha256) and [after-refusal](rerun-stale-source.sha256) hashes, [original validation](rerun-validation-1.json), [fresh validation](rerun-validation-2.json), [fresh approval](rerun-approvals-2.json), [final policy](workspace/policy.json), and [post-application checker result](rerun-independent-check.json). The [original audit](audit.json) and its [script](audit.py) are included, along with the [recorded Lexsi source revision](lexsi-head.txt).

For publication, occurrences of the original home directory in the two proposal, validation and approval records were replaced with `<redacted-home>`; no other content in those files was changed. The hashes in the original audit describe the unredacted original records, so they do not match these redacted files. The script writes `audit.json` if run; preserve the original before replaying it. The records are a retained witness, not a complete portable Workbench deployment or a new inference run.
